# Surface Brief — Homepage (Skynet landing)

## Scope and visitor mode
Persuade surface. Visitor decides whether Skynet is for them and acts: signs up and describes a site in plain language.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Audience: non-technical people (vibe-coders) AND technical builders who want a no-code path.
- Job: get a live, professional website without writing code, designing, or configuring servers.
- Action: sign up/log in, then describe the site they want in plain language.
- Proof: "no code required" badge; the agent-team workflow (research → plan → design → build → test → secure → deploy); deploy to a real `<project-id>.skynet.app` URL.
- Constraints: no bitmap terminal font (alienates non-coders), no hacker jargon, clear primary action within 5 seconds, WCAG AA contrast.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
Cracktro Scroller — Approachable (grounded candidate, safer register). Emissive black void ground (#0A0A0A), signal amber (#FFB900) as the single active-state accent, clean white readable type (not bitmap) riding baselines, gentle neutral dust drift behind content. Memorable moment: the single amber CTA button against the black void, with the headline "Build your website by describing it."

## Direction contract
THESIS: Skynet is a no-code website builder whose landing page feels like capable software, not a retro music poster or a generic SaaS template. Refuses the category-default centered-headline-over-equal-cards layout and refuses the edgy terminal look that locks out non-coders.
OWN-WORLD: Emissive black void as ground; signal amber as the sole saturated accent used only for active state and the primary CTA; clean white/off-white readable sans-serif body; graphite/dust neutrals (ash to ivory) for secondary text and background drift; depth expressed through brightness steps and letterspacing, never boxes, rules, or dividers.
STORY: The visitor understands within seconds that Skynet builds websites from plain language, believes it's for them (not just hackers), and acts by clicking the amber "Build my website" button.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black void. Hero headline "Build your website by describing it" at display scale, left-aligned. Subcopy beneath it. One prominent amber primary button "Build my website" below subcopy. Secondary "Log in" link. Gentle dust drift behind, layered speeds, nothing contained by boxes. Primary action sits in lower-third of first viewport, unmistakable.
