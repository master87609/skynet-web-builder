# Requirements Specification — Synapse AI
Project: Artificial Intelligence & Automation
Client Concept: "Autonomous drone delivery network for medical organs between hospital helipads with real-time flight telemetry and cold-chain monitoring"

## 1. Executive Vision
Build an exceptional, high-converting digital platform for **Synapse AI** in the **Artificial Intelligence & Automation** sector.

## 2. Functional Requirements
- **FR-01 (Brand Identity & Hero):** Bold hero introducing "The next-generation artificial intelligence & automation experience designed for precision and results." with dual primary/secondary action triggers.
- **FR-02 (Core Offerings):** Showcase grid presenting Autonomous Suite, Drone Engine, Delivery Hub, Network Cloud.
- **FR-03 (Interactive Conversion Module):** Interactive modal dialogue allowing visitors to submit inquiries or book appointments with instant client-side validation.
- **FR-04 (Social Validation):** Verified testimonials and performance metrics establishing immediate market credibility.
- **FR-05 (FAQ Accordion):** Searchable accordion addressing high-intent buyer concerns.
- **FR-06 (Responsive Viewports):** Touch-optimized mobile navigation drawer and desktop bar.

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements
- **NFR-01 (Speed):** First Contentful Paint < 0.6s via CDN utility distribution.
- **NFR-02 (A11y):** WCAG 2.2 AA compliant contrast on dark void canvas.
- **NFR-03 (Zero Dependencies):** Self-contained web application requiring zero broken third-party servers.
