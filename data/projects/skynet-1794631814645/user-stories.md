# User Stories: Cinematic Shoe Website

*   **US-01: Cinematic 3D Exploration**
    *   **As a** Sneakerhead visitor,
    *   **I want** the 3D shoe model to rotate and break apart into its component layers as I scroll down the page,
    *   **So that** I can visually appreciate the craftsmanship and design engineering of the footwear in an interactive way.

*   **US-02: Material & Technology Deep Dive**
    *   **As a** potential buyer,
    *   **I want** to click on highlighted hotspots on the shoe during my scroll journey,
    *   **So that** I can read detailed specs about the specialized materials and cushioning technology used.

*   **US-03: Colorway Customization**
    *   **As an** aesthetic-driven shopper,
    *   **I want** to switch between different colorways and material finishes (e.g., Cyberpunk Neon, Stealth Black, Minimalist White) in real-time on the 3D model,
    *   **So that** I can visualize my preferred style before purchasing.

*   **US-04: Cinematic Audio-Visual Atmosphere**
    *   **As a** design enthusiast,
    *   **I want** ambient sound effects and synchronized kinetic typography to accompany my scroll,
    *   **So that** I am fully immersed in a cinematic product launch experience.

*   **US-05: Frictionless Mobile Experience**
    *   **As a** mobile user browsing on my phone,
    *   **I want** the scroll-motion experience to adapt smoothly to touch gestures without lagging or crashing my browser,
    *   **So that** I can enjoy the high-end presentation seamlessly on mobile.

*   **US-06: Instant Express Checkout**
    *   **As an** impulse buyer ready to purchase,
    *   **I want** to trigger a quick-checkout drawer with Apple Pay integration directly from the final scroll chapter,
    *   **So that** I can complete my order in under 30 seconds without navigating away from the cinematic experience.
