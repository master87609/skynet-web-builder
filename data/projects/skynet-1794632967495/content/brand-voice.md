# Skynet Brand Voice & Editorial Guidelines — Project Chronos

## 1. Brand Essence & Positioning
Chronos is the convergence of avant-garde high fashion and spatial computing. It occupies the intersection of brutalist architecture, Japanese technical textiles, and cyber-luxury. The voice is unapologetic, precise, evocative, and uncompromisingly modern. We do not sell clothes; we architect silhouettes and engineer wearable environments.

## 2. Core Tonal Pillars
- **Cinematic & Atmospheric:** Language should evoke motion, shadow, light, and temporal flow. Use terms that feel tactile, immersive, and weighty.
- **Architectural & Technical:** Frame garments through engineering, structural integrity, and material science (e.g., carbon weave, technical silk, tension hardware).
- **Elite & Restrained:** Never loud or desperate. The tone whispers luxury through clarity and confidence. Minimalist yet intensely evocative.

## 3. Vocabulary & Lexicon
- **Embrace:** *Spatial, Silhouette, Chronology, Obsidian, Monolith, Synthesis, Atmosphere, Kinetic, Structural, Tailored, Immutable.*
- **Avoid:** *Trendy, Cheap, Sale, Awesome, Check out, Cool, Fast fashion, Click here.*

## 4. Key Slogans & Micro-Copy
- *Hero Header:* "THE ARCHITECTURE OF SILHOUETTE"
- *Sub-headline:* "Scroll to descend into the spatial runway. Explore garments crafted from Japanese technical silk and carbon-infused wool in real-time 3D space."
- *Action Prompt:* "BEGIN DESCENT" / "INSPECT LOOK 01" / "SECURE GARMENT"
