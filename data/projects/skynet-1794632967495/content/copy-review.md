# Skynet Copy Review & Editorial Optimization

An editorial audit of `code/index.html` was conducted to elevate luxury positioning, improve call-to-action (CTA) urgency, and eliminate generic e-commerce phrasing in favor of our cinematic-architectural brand voice.

## 1. Global Navigation & Header
* **Original:** `BAG` (Counter: 0)
* **Optimized:** `ARCHIVE BAG` / `DOSSIER`
* **Rationale:** Aligns transactional elements with the archival, museum-grade aesthetic of a high-end digital runway.

## 2. Preloader & Audio Prompt
* **Original:** "Experience the capsule collection with spatial acoustic soundscape." + "Enable Audio & Enter" / "Mute & Enter"
* **Optimized:** "Initiate spatial acoustic immersion alongside the FW25 runway." + "ACTIVATE SOUNDSCAPE & ENTER" / "SILENT DESCENT"
* **Rationale:** Replaces standard prompt language with atmospheric, deliberate command verbs that reinforce the cinematic mood.

## 3. Hero Section (Act I)
* **Original:** 
  - Badge: `AUTUMN / WINTER 2025 // 3D CINEMATIC EXPERIENCE`
  - Headline: `THE ARCHITECTURE OF SILHOUETTE`
  - CTA: `BEGIN DESCENT` / `INSPECT LOOK 01`
* **Optimized:** 
  - Badge: `FW25 CAPSULE // SPATIAL RUNWAY MATRIX`
  - Headline: `THE ARCHITECTURE OF SILHOUETTE` *(Retained: High brand alignment)*
  - CTA: `INITIATE DESCENT` / `ANALYZE LOOK 01`
* **Rationale:** Upgrades generic terms like "Begin" and "Inspect" to high-tech architectural terminology ("Initiate", "Analyze").

## 4. Collection Section (Act II / Hotspots)
* **Original:** 
  - Section Header: `CHRONOS CAPSULE PIECES` -> Sub: "Interact with spatial hotspots or select any garment for 360-degree material analysis."
  - Button: `Quick Inspection ($480)`
* **Optimized:** 
  - Section Header: `FW25 ARCHITECTURAL MANIFESTATIONS` -> Sub: "Engage spatial coordinates or invoke 360-degree material telemetry for each garment."
  - Button: `VIEW TELEMETRY ($480)`
* **Rationale:** Shifts product browsing into an immersive technical analysis workflow that matches WebGL spatial interaction mechanics.
