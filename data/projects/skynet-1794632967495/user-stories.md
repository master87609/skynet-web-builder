# user-stories.md

## User Stories: Project Chronos-Garment

### User Story 1 (Cinematic Experience)
**As a** trendsetting consumer,
**I want** the website to act as a smooth, scroll-driven cinematic runway,
**So that** I feel immersed in a high-fashion digital art piece rather than browsing a boring, static online store.

### User Story 2 (Product Inspection)
**As a** fashion enthusiast,
**I want** to click on a clothing item during the scroll sequence to pause the camera and rotate the 3D model in 360 degrees,
**So that** I can thoroughly examine the fabric texture, stitching details, and garment silhouette before making a purchase decision.

### User Story 3 (Contextual Shopping)
**As a** shopper ready to buy,
**I want** to see subtle interactive hotspots on the 3D garments that reveal pricing, available colors, and sizing,
**So that** I can instantly understand product details without breaking the cinematic immersion.

### User Story 4 (Express Purchase)
**As a** mobile user with Apple Pay enabled,
**I want** to add a garment to my cart and complete checkout through a fast slide-over drawer,
**So that** I can secure limited-release items instantly without navigating away from the 3D experience.

### User Story 5 (Accessibility & Control)
**As a** user who is sensitive to rapid motion or browsing in a quiet environment,
**I want** easy-to-use toggle controls to mute the ambient soundtrack and switch to a reduced-motion or standard layout mode,
**So that** I can comfortably navigate the website according to my personal preferences and physical needs.

### User Story 6 (Brand Director / Marketing)
**As a** brand director,
**I want** the website to deliver lightning-fast initial load times and smooth 60 FPS transitions via optimized 3D asset streaming,
**So that** high-value visitors are immediately captivated by the brand's prestige and do not bounce due to lag.
