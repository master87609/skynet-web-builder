# User Stories: Skynet E-Commerce Platform ("Apparel Hub")

*   **User Story 1: Product Discovery and Filtering**
    *   **As a** Trendy Shopper (Chloe),
    *   **I want** to filter the clothing catalog by size, color, and price range,
    *   **So that** I can quickly find items that fit my style and preferences without scrolling through irrelevant products.

*   **User Story 2: Secure User Registration**
    *   **As a** new site visitor,
    *   **I want** to create an account using my email or social login (Google/Apple),
    *   **So that** I can securely save my shipping addresses and view my past orders.

*   **User Story 3: Streamlined Shopping Cart Management**
    *   **As a** shopper,
    *   **I want** to add items, change quantities, or remove products from my shopping cart and see the subtotal update instantly,
    *   **So that** I can review my purchases clearly before proceeding to payment.

*   **User Story 4: Express Checkout Experience**
    *   **As a** mobile-first shopper,
    *   **I want** to use express payment methods (like Apple Pay or credit card autofill),
    *   **So that** I can complete my clothing purchase in under a minute without manually typing long forms.

*   **User Story 5: Order Status Notifications**
    *   **As a** customer who just placed an order,
    *   **I want** to receive an automated email confirmation containing my tracking link and order summary,
    *   **So that** I have peace of mind and can track the delivery of my new clothes.

*   **User Story 6: Administrative Inventory Management**
    *   **As a** Store Administrator (Marcus),
    *   **I want** to add new clothing items, upload product photos, and update inventory stock counts via an admin dashboard,
    *   **So that** our online catalog remains accurate and up-to-date for customers.
