Our Heritage & Craft

Time-Honored Fermentation Meets Modern Precision.

At Skynet Bakehouse, we believe true sourdough cannot be rushed. Our dough undergoes a meticulous 36-to-48 hour cold fermentation process that breaks down complex starches and gluten proteins, resulting in exceptional flavor complexity and effortless digestibility.

1

Stone-Milled Organic Grains

We source our unbleached wheat, spelt, and rye exclusively from regional regenerative organic farms committed to soil health.

2

Wild Yeast Culture

Our mother starter has been nurtured daily for over a decade, imparting a signature complex sour note with delicate floral undertones.

3

Deck-Oven Steam Baking

Every loaf is baked directly on hearth stone with precise steam injection to achieve a caramelized, blistering crunchy crust.

Support & Logistics

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our weekly drops, pickups, and sourdough care.

Our weekly menu drops every Thursday at 6:00 PM PST. Quantities are strictly limited based on our daily baking capacity to ensure pristine artisanal quality. You select your items, choose a Friday or Saturday pickup window, and complete checkout. Once sold out, items cannot be restocked until the following week.
Pickups take place at our micro-bakery located at 742 Evergreen Terrace during your selected 1-hour time slot (e.g., Friday 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM). Please show your digital order confirmation QR code upon arrival for rapid handoff.
Store your loaf cut-side down on a wooden cutting board or in a linen bread bag at room temperature for up to 4 days. Avoid plastic bags, which soften the crust. To revive day-3 bread, lightly mist the crust with water and bake in a preheated 400°F oven for 6–8 minutes.
While our long 36-hour fermentation breaks down a significant portion of gluten proteins (making our bread much easier to digest for many people with mild sensitivities), our bakery handles organic wheat, spelt, rye, and dairy. Therefore, our products are not suitable for individuals with celiac disease.
