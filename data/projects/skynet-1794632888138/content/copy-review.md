# Copy Review & Editorial Audit: Skynet Bakehouse Website

## 1. Executive Audit Summary
An editorial audit of `code/index.html` reveals a strong visual foundation and robust UI implementation, but the current copy lacks the emotional pull and sensory vocabulary required for high-end artisanal food e-commerce. This review provides high-impact headline refactors, micro-copy enhancements, and conversion-optimized Call-to-Action (CTA) rewrites to maximize Friday drop conversions.

---

## 2. Section-by-Section Copy Optimization

### A. Navigation & Header Badges
* **Current Copy:** `Friday Drop Live: 42 Loaves Left`
* **Critique:** Functional, but lacks emotional urgency.
* **Optimized Copy:** `🔥 Friday Batch #104 Live: Only 42 Loaves Remaining`
* **Rationale:** Adding the batch number enhances authenticity and collector appeal, while the flame icon triggers immediate visual attention.

### B. Hero Section
* **Current Hero Headline:** `Wild Ferment. Blistered Crust.`
  * *Optimized Polish:* Retained, as it is punchy and sensory-driven.
* **Current Hero Paragraph:** `Handcrafted artisanal sourdough baked in micro-batches using ancient heritage grains, 36-hour cold fermentation, and organic unbleached flour. Secure your weekly loaf before the drop sells out.`
  * *Optimized Copy:* `Hand-shaped over 36 hours using stone-milled organic grains and wild yeasts. Experience an open, gelatinized crumb and deeply caramelized crust. Secure your weekly loaf before the Friday batch is fully claimed.`
* **Current Hero CTA Buttons:** 
  * Primary: `Shop Weekly Drop` $\rightarrow$ **Optimized:** `Secure Your Weekly Loaf`
  * Secondary: `Our Fermentation Story` $\rightarrow$ **Optimized:** `Discover Our 36-Hour Process`

### C. Weekly Menu & Preorder Hub Section
* **Current Section Header:** `Baking Schedule & Available Loaves`
* **Optimized Header:** `The Friday & Saturday Preorder Menu`
* **Current Subheader:** `Select your dietary preferences and secure your artisanal bakes for upcoming Friday & Saturday pickups.`
* **Optimized Subheader:** `Hand-crafted in micro-batches. Reserve your pickup slot below before our stone ovens reach capacity.`

### D. Product Card Micro-Copy & CTAs
* **Current Product Button:** `Add to Preorder`
* **Optimized Product Button:** `Reserve This Loaf`
* **Current Low-Stock Warning:** `Only 4 left!`
* **Optimized Low-Stock Warning:** `⚠️ Only 4 loaves left in Batch #104`

---

## 3. Implementation Recommendations for `code/index.html`

1. **Replace Generic Verbs:** Audit all instances of "Buy" and "Cart" across modals and drawers, shifting toward "Reserve," "Preorder," and "Your Bread Box."
2. **Inject Sensory Metrics:** Ensure every product card prominently highlights its fermentation time (e.g., *36h Cold Retard*) to reinforce justification for artisanal pricing.
3. **Enhance Micro-Interactions:** Update toast notifications to sound like a local baker confirming an order: *“Your sourdough is resting safely in our reservation queue.”*
