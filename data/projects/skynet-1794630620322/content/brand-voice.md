# Skynet Brand Voice & Editorial Guidelines: Alex Rivers Portfolio

## 1. Brand Essence & Positioning
* **Archetype:** The Pragmatic Innovator & Systems Craftsman
* **Core Value Proposition:** Alex Rivers bridges the gap between deep-tech engineering and high-impact business outcomes. The brand voice is confident, authoritative, unpretentious, and razor-sharp.
* **Mission:** To communicate elite technical capability without unnecessary jargon, demonstrating reliability, speed, and architectural mastery to recruiters, engineering leaders, and enterprise clients.

---

## 2. Tone Pillars
1. **Precision-Engineered:** Every word serves a purpose. Avoid fluff, filler adverbs (*"very," "really"*), and generic corporate buzzwords (*"synergy," "ninja"*). 
2. **Authoritative & Calm:** Speak from a position of mastery. Use active voice and concrete metrics (e.g., *“processes 500k event streams/sec”* instead of *“handles lots of data”*).
3. **Approachable & Collaborative:** While deeply technical, Alex is a team player. The tone remains welcoming, inviting dialogue and collaboration rather than talking down to the audience.

---

## 3. Vocabulary & Lexicon

| Category | Preferred Terms | Avoid / Banned Terms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engineering** | Resilient, distributed, high-performance, production-grade, modular, scalable | Rock-star, guru, ninja, cutting-edge, bleeding-edge (too cliché) |
| **Action / Output** | Architecting, building, shipping, optimizing, deploying, scaling | Working on, doing, making stuff |
| **Quality** | Sub-100ms latency, robust, enterprise-grade, tested | Good, fast, nice, cool |

---

## 4. Key Slogans & Micro-Copy

* **Hero Subheader Hook:** *"Architecting resilient web systems & high-impact apps."*
* **Terminal / Config Tagline:** *"Ready to build."*
* **Availability Badge:** *"Available for Senior Roles & Architecture Consulting"*
* **Resume CTA:** *"Get CV"* / *"Download Verified CV"*

---

## 5. Formatting & Stylistic Rules
* **Code Integration:** Where technical syntax appears (e.g., `alex.config.ts`), keep comments clean, modern, and aligned with TypeScript/JSON conventions.
* **Metric Formatting:** Always use exact metrics when available (e.g., *8+ years*, *1,428 commits*, *sub-100ms latency*). Quantifiable proof builds immediate trust with technical recruiters.
