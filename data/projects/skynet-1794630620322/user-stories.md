# User Stories

* **US-01: First Impression & Overview**
  * **As a** hiring manager visiting the portfolio for the first time,  
  * **I want** to immediately see a professional headline, a clear summary of who the client is, and easy access to their resume,  
  * **So that** I can instantly determine if their core expertise matches my open role.

* **US-02: Project Evaluation**
  * **As a** technical recruiter,  
  * **I want** to browse through a categorized collection of past projects with tech stack tags and links to live demos/code repositories,  
  * **So that** I can verify the quality and practical application of the client's coding and design skills.

* **US-03: Career History Review**
  * **As a** prospective employer,  
  * **I want** to view a clean chronological timeline of the client's work experience and professional background,  
  * **So that** I can understand their career trajectory and past responsibilities.

* **US-04: Direct Communication**
  * **As a** potential client with a project proposal,  
  * **I want** to fill out a simple and validated contact form directly on the site,  
  * **So that** I can easily reach out to the portfolio owner without needing to open my external email client.

* **US-05: Resume Access**
  * **As a** recruiter who needs to pass candidate materials upstream to stakeholders,  
  * **I want** to download the client's up-to-date CV as a PDF with a single click,  
  * **So that** I can attach it easily to internal hiring candidate profiles.

* **US-06: Visual Comfort Preference**
  * **As a** visitor browsing the site in a low-light environment,  
  * **I want** to toggle the website theme from light mode to dark mode,  
  * **So that** my eyes are protected from harsh screen glare and I can enjoy a comfortable viewing experience.
