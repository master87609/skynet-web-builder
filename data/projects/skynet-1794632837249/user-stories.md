# Skynet User Stories: Artisanal Sourdough Bakery with Preorders

## User Story 1: Preorder Batch Browsing
**As a** Conscious Foodie,  
**I want** to view the upcoming week's specialty sourdough menu with scheduled pickup dates,  
**So that** I can plan my weekend meals and secure limited-batch loaves before they sell out.

## User Story 2: Real-Time Inventory & Checkout Lock
**As a** Conscious Foodie,  
**I want** the system to temporarily hold my chosen loaf in my cart during checkout,  
**So that** I do not lose my item to another customer while entering my payment details.

## User Story 3: Flexible Pickup Slot Reservation
**As a** Conscious Foodie,  
**I want** to select a specific 30-minute pickup window on baking day,  
**So that** I can collect my warm bread efficiently without waiting in long queues.

## User Story 4: Automated Order Confirmation & Reminders
**As a** Conscious Foodie,  
**I want** to receive an instant email confirmation and a reminder text before my pickup time,  
**So that** I never forget to collect my fresh bread.

## User Story 5: Production Cap & Batch Management
**As a** Master Baker,  
**I want** to set strict maximum inventory limits for each dough type per baking day,  
**So that** my kitchen team is never overwhelmed by over-sold orders beyond our daily flour and oven capacity.

## User Story 6: Kitchen Fulfillment Dashboard
**As a** Master Baker,  
**I want** to view and print a consolidated daily fulfillment sheet organized by pickup times,  
**So that** my staff can efficiently pack orders and hand them to customers at the counter.
