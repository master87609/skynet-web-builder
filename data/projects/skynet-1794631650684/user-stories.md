# Skynet User Stories: Apple Cinematic Experience

*   **US-01: Cinematic Assembly Sequence**
    *   As a Design Aficionado, I want flagship products to dynamically assemble piece-by-piece as I scroll down the page so that I can visually appreciate the internal engineering and minimalist architecture.
*   **US-02: 3D Product Exploration**
    *   As an Enterprise Decision Maker, I want to freely rotate and inspect the device in 3D with realistic lighting and reflections so that I can evaluate the physical build quality before making a purchase decision.
*   **US-03: Reduced Motion Accessibility**
    *   As a user with vestibular sensitivity, I want the website to automatically detect my system-level "Reduce Motion" preference and provide a static, elegant editorial layout so that I can explore the content without experiencing motion sickness.
*   **US-04: Ambient Soundscape Control**
    *   As an immersive media consumer, I want an option to enable subtle spatial audio and sound effects during my journey so that the cinematic atmosphere is heightened without being intrusive.
*   **US-05: Macro-Detail Inspection**
    *   As a curious technologist, I want to click on interactive hotspots on the 3D model to zoom into microscopic details like the chassis texture and lens elements so that I understand the precision manufacturing.
*   **US-06: Frictionless Purchase Transition**
    *   As a ready buyer, I want to transition smoothly from the cinematic climax directly into a product configuration and checkout modal so that I can purchase the item without breaking the immersive flow.
